2 · Editing a part
Everything about the part, in place
Tap a card to open it: name (a dropdown of common names, or your own), length in bars, tempo (and "Gradual tempo change" to give it an end tempo, changed a little on every beat), its rhythm (opens the same Rhythm panel), its accents, and Play from here.

3 · Main screen with parts
The ruler becomes the song map
Same feel as the tempo ruler: the red needle stays in the middle and the song map moves under it as the song plays. Slide it or tap a part to go there (snaps to the part's start, on the next bar line). −5…+5 and Tap change the current part only.
