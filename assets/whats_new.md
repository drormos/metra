# What's new in Metra

Newest first. The top version's lines are also the Play Store release notes.

## 1.9.0 · Nov 2026
- New: a tuner for guitar and bass. Tap the tuning fork at the top.
- The click is louder at full volume.
- The time signature button is now a small square that never changes size.
- Tap anywhere outside a panel to close it.

## 1.8.0 · Oct 2026
- Send a whole setlist or song to a bandmate: Share › Export, they copy it and tap Share › Import.
- Practice speed climbs in smaller steps: +1%, +2%, +2.5% and up.

## 1.7.0 · Oct 2026
- Song parts are at the top of the 4/4 panel, and you can build them right in Free play, then save them as a song.
- Practice speed for songs with parts: play the whole song at 80% (or 110%) and speed up each time it loops.
- Share a setlist: send the running order with tempos to your band.
- Simpler time signatures: any count over 4 or 8, plus one-tap 3/4, 4/4, 6/8 and 12/8.

## 1.6.0 · Oct 2026
- Song parts: tempo, time-signature and gradual changes inside a song. Open the 4/4 button › Song parts, or hold the song's name.
- Stage mode: your set full screen, for gigs
- The screen stays on while Metra is open (Settings)
- The song map: slide or tap to jump to a part
- Every stepper repeats when held and takes typed numbers

## 1.5.3 · Oct 2026
- Count-in, speed up and stop after, from the stopwatch
- Subdivision now lives in the Rhythm panel ("4/4")
- Settings replaces the menu (gear, top right)
- Panels open from the button you tap and close with a swipe
- A bolder app icon
- Your songs and settings are saved more safely
- Steadier click when you change something right before a bar line
