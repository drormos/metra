# Metra privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026.

Metra is a metronome. It has no ads, no account and no tracking, and your songs stay on your phone.

**What Metra collects:** nothing. The app has no internet access (it doesn't request Android's internet permission), so it can't send anything anywhere. There is no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising code in it.

**What stays on your phone:** your songs, setlists and settings, saved in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

**Android backup:** if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include Metra's songs and settings in your Google account backup, so they come back on a new phone. That backup is run by Android and Google under your Google account; Metra doesn't see or control it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

**Sending feedback:** "Send feedback" in Settings opens your own email app with a message to the developer that starts with Metra's version, your phone model and your Android version. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. Emails are used only to reply and to improve the app, and aren't shared.

**Permissions:** Metra asks for no permission you have to approve. It uses Android's media playback service so the click keeps playing with the screen off.

**Children:** Metra collects no data from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.

**Contact:** metra.application@gmail.com
